What is Paevos?

A money assistant for iPhone. It connects to your bank, budgets your day, answers your questions, and tracks your progress toward savings and debt goals.

Do I have to enter expenses?

Never. Connect your bank once and every transaction comes in on its own.

Can Paevos move my money?

No. The bank connection is read-only. Paevos can see balances and transactions, never move money.

How is my daily budget worked out?

Plain arithmetic. Your real balance, minus bills due before payday, divided by the days left. No guessing.

When does Paevos launch?

When it's ready, not before. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the day it's on the App Store.

Is it iPhone only?

Yes, at launch. Paevos is built native for iOS.

Is this financial advice?

No. Paevos shows how things fit your own numbers. The decisions are always yours.
